* **The Mission**: "I help Philippine BPOs protect their margins by brokering the integration of Western AI efficiency tools into their local human workflows".
* **The Arbitrage**: Connect US-based AI developers with local BPO firms. Fee: "Transformation Retainer" or percentage of OpEx savings.

## 2. Solar PPA (Power Purchase Agreement) Scout
* **The Problem**: High electricity costs in the Philippines cripple commercial growth, but local businesses lack the capital for solar hardware.
* **The Mission**: "I connect commercial property owners with global green-energy funds to facilitate zero-upfront-cost solar installations via Power Purchase Agreements".
* **The Arbitrage**: Act as a "Green Broker." Finder's fee from investment fund upon contract signing + micro-commission per kilowatt-hour sold.

## 3. Agri-Logistics Intelligence Broker
* **The Problem**: Significant price gaps exist between provincial farm gates and Manila retail due to information asymmetry and fragmented logistics.
* **The Mission**: "I optimize agricultural supply chains by connecting provincial cooperatives directly to urban retail clusters using data-driven logistics scheduling".
* **The Arbitrage**: Manage information flow. "Intelligence Fee" per shipment for coordinating timing and bulk-orders to eliminate middlemen.

## 4. Specialized Remote Talent Scout
* **The Problem**: Western firms are moving away from generalist VAs and seeking "Specialized Operators" (AI Prompt Engineers, Technical Sales, etc.).
* **The Mission**: "I curate and broker elite Philippine technical talent to Western firms seeking high-impact, specialized remote operations".
* **The Arbitrage**: Scout top 1% local specialists. Package as a "Performance Unit" and broker for a 20% ongoing management commission.
