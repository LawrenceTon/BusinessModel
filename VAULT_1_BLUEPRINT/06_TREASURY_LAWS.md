# 06_TREASURY_LAWS: THE ARITHMETIC OF DOMINANCE

## I. THE 10% SEED (THE BABYLON LAW)
* [cite_start]A part of all you earn is yours to keep[cite: 50].
* [cite_start]Exactly 10% of every peso acquired via "Finder's Fees" or "Consulting" must be set aside immediately[cite: 51, 92].
* [cite_start]This capital is the "Seed" specifically reserved for the future FDA License to Operate (LTO)[cite: 12, 168].

## II. THE 70% LIMIT (OPERATIONAL CEILING)
* [cite_start]Personal living expenses, including food and clothing, must never exceed 70% of total income[cite: 52, 169].
* [cite_start]This enforces the "Lean and Flexible" mindset required for zero-capital scaling[cite: 194].

## III. THE 20% DEBT & GROWTH ACCELERATOR
* [cite_start]The remaining 20% of income is dedicated to systematic debt eradication and reinvestment into empire tools (laptops, software, connectivity)[cite: 53, 29].

## IV. WEALTH FORMULA
* [cite_start]Wealth = (Income - Expenses) × Time + (Capital × Compound Interest)[cite: 91].
